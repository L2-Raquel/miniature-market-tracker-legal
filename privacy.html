Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy explains what data Miniature Market Tracker (the "Service") collects, why, and how it is handled. The Service is distributed through the RapidAPI marketplace.

Not affiliated with Games Workshop. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Games Workshop. Warhammer is a trademark of Games Workshop Limited.

1. Summary

The Service is a data API. It does not ask you to create an account with us, and we do not store personal information about the people who call the API.

2. What we collect ourselves

In normal operation we store no personal data: no names, no email addresses, no API keys and no billing details. Requests are handled transiently in memory to produce a response and are not retained.

Our hosting and infrastructure providers (application hosting and the managed database provider) keep short-lived technical logs such as IP address, timestamp, requested path, response code and latency, for security, troubleshooting and capacity monitoring. These logs are not used to build profiles or advertising audiences.

3. Data we publish as the product

The Service's dataset consists of eBay listing information (title, price, currency, condition, image and URL) captured automatically via the official eBay Browse API, together with statistics derived from it. This listing content originates from eBay and may include text or images submitted by third-party sellers. We do not publish personal data of individual eBay sellers; seller contact details are not collected or exposed by the API.

4. Subscriptions and billing

Subscription accounts, payment and API key management are provided by RapidAPI under its own privacy policy and terms. We do not receive or process your payment card data. We see only non-identifying operational information about your subscription, such as plan name and request volume, as needed to operate and support the Service.

5. Cookies and tracking

The Service's responses set no cookies and run no advertising or cross-site tracking. This website (these legal pages) uses no analytics scripts and no cookies.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for advertising. Data may be processed by our infrastructure providers acting as processors (application hosting and database hosting), and disclosed where required by law or to protect rights and safety.

7. International transfers

Our infrastructure providers operate globally, so technical logs and service data may be processed outside your country of residence, under the safeguards those providers apply.

8. Retention

Technical logs are kept only as long as needed for security and operations. Market data is retained as part of the historical dataset. Personal data is not retained.

9. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data, we generally cannot identify or retrieve information about an individual API user. Requests to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to processing, can be raised through the contact channel published on the Service's RapidAPI page. If your request concerns your RapidAPI account itself, RapidAPI handles it.

10. Children

The Service is a business-to-business data API and is not directed to children under 16.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance.
