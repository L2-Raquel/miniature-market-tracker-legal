1. What the Service provides

The Service offers programmatic access to a curated database of miniature collector market data. Each day we query the official eBay Browse API for tracked models, filter and normalise the results, and store a daily price history. Endpoints expose a per-model snapshot, a price history series, and a market activity summary.

2. Nature of the data

All figures reflect active asking prices (current listings) collected from eBay. They are not completed sale prices, and they are not an official eBay product. Prices are shown in the currency returned by eBay and, where available, converted to EUR using a periodically updated fixed exchange rate. Medians and deal scores are statistical measures computed from our own captured data and may not represent what a buyer would ultimately pay.

Model coverage is curated by hand. Availability changes as our catalogue evolves, and any endpoint may return fewer or no records for a model at times.
